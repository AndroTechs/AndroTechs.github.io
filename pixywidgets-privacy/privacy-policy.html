Privacy Policy

Pixy Widgets respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when using the app.

Information Collection

Pixy Widgets does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app may collect limited non-personal information automatically for analytics, crash reporting, and improving app performance.

Pixy Widgets provides customizable widgets, wallpapers, and themes for personalization purposes only. Any generated or downloaded assets are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated.

Advertising

The app may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses information here: Google Advertising Policies

Third-Party Services

Pixy Widgets may use trusted third-party services for:

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable methods to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

Pixy Widgets is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
