Delete Account — DentiSet

This page explains how users of DentiSet can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the DentiSet app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your request

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us via email:

Email: androtech@bk.ru

What Data is Deleted

  • User account information
  • Patient records
  • Appointments and clinic data

Data Retention

All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of the request, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
