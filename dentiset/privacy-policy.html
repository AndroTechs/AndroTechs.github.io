🔒 Privacy Policy — DentiSet

App Name: DentiSet
Developer: AndroTechs Apps

1. Introduction

DentiSet (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a dental clinic management application designed to help dentists and clinic staff manage patients, appointments, and clinic operations efficiently.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

2. Data Collection & Usage

DentiSet may store and process the following types of data, depending on how the app is used:

  • Patient information (e.g., name, phone number, notes)
  • Appointment data (date, time, status)
  • Clinic-related records (services, treatments, history)

This data is entered by the user (clinic owner or staff) and is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app.

We do NOT collect or access any data without user action.

3. Data Storage

  • All data is stored securely
  • Data may be stored:
    • Locally on the device
    • Or on secure cloud services (e.g., Firebase), if enabled

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

4. Authentication & Accounts

DentiSet may require user authentication (e.g., email/password or Google Sign-In) to:

  • Secure access to clinic data
  • Sync data across devices

Authentication data is handled securely via trusted providers (such as Firebase Authentication).

5. Permissions

DentiSet may request certain permissions only when needed, such as:

  • Internet access → for syncing and backups
  • Storage (if applicable) → for exporting or saving data

Permissions are used strictly for app functionality and never for tracking.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Secure network communication (HTTPS)
  • Authentication protection
  • Access control within the app

However, no system is 100% secure, and users are encouraged to keep their login credentials safe.

7. Patient Data Responsibility

DentiSet acts as a tool for clinics.

The clinic owner or user is fully responsible for:

  • The data they enter
  • Ensuring compliance with local data protection laws
  • Obtaining patient consent where required

We do not independently verify or control user-entered data.

8. Children’s Privacy

DentiSet is not intended for use by children.
We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Modify or delete your data
  • Stop using the service at any time

You can delete your data directly within the app, by deleting your account, or by contacting us to request data deletion.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be reflected on this page.
